How to save this to your Google Disk / Sheets:

  1. Click the Export to Google Sheets (CSV) button at the top right to download Japan_Prebooked_Attractions.csv.
  2. Open your Google Drive (Google Disk) and navigate to your preferred trip folder.
  3. Drag and drop the downloaded CSV file into your Google Drive folder.
  4. Right-click the uploaded file in Google Drive, select Open with → Google Sheets. It will automatically convert into a fully editable Google Sheet!
